# Superstore Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
Analysis of 9,994 Superstore orders (2014-2017) to understand sales, profit, discounts, regions and customers. Built with Google Sheets (pivot tables and charts).

## Business Questions
1. How are sales and profit changing over time?
2. Which categories and sub-categories earn the most profit or lose money?
3. Which regions and states are underperforming?
4. Does higher discount reduce profit?
5. Who are the top customers?

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: $286,397.02
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Total Orders: 5,009

## Key Findings
- Discounts above 30% lead to losses.
- Technology earns the most profit. Furniture has high sales but low profit.
- Tables and Supplies are loss-making sub-categories (verify Bookcases against your pivot table).
- West is the strongest region. Texas and Illinois lose money, with average discounts above 30%.
- The top customer by sales is loss-making.
- In 2014 and 2017 discounts were higher and margin was lower; in 2015 and 2016 the reverse.

## Recommendations
- Cap discounts at 30%.
- Review pricing and discounts for Tables and Bookcases.
- Control discounts in Texas and Illinois.
- Rank customers by profit, not only sales.
